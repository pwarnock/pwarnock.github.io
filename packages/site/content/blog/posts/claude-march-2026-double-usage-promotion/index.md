Anthropic is running a promotion from **March 13-27** that doubles your five-hour usage limits during off-peak hours. No action required — it's automatic.

## The Details

- **Off-peak** (doubled usage): Outside 8 AM–2 PM ET / 5–11 AM PT
- **Peak** (standard rates): 8 AM–2 PM ET / 5–11 AM PT
- **Eligible plans**: Free, Pro, Max, and Team (not Enterprise)
- **Where**: Claude web, mobile, desktop, Cowork, Claude Code, Claude for Excel, Claude for PowerPoint
- Bonus usage doesn't count toward weekly limits

## Why This Matters

If you're a heavy Claude Code user, this is a good excuse to shift your longer sessions to evenings or early mornings for the next two weeks. The off-peak window is generous — basically anything outside a six-hour block in the middle of the US business day.

Worth noting: this is the first time Anthropic has run a usage promotion like this. Could be testing demand elasticity, could be smoothing load, could be building goodwill. Either way, free capacity is free capacity.
